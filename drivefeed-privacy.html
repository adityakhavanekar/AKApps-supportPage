Privacy Policy

Drive Feed - Last Updated: February 4, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Drive Feed ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our iOS application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Information We Collect

Our app may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Phone number and username when you create an account
  • Camera/Photo Data: Images you capture or select for car identification
  • Location Data: Your location when you spot a car (used to tag where the car was spotted)
  • User-Generated Content: Captions, comments, and likes on car spots
  • Usage Analytics: App usage statistics to improve performance and user experience
  • Device Information: Basic device and iOS version information for compatibility
  • Subscription Data: Purchase and subscription information managed by Apple

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Provide core app functionality (AI-powered car recognition and social features)
  • Display your car spots on the community feed
  • Maintain leaderboard rankings and points system
  • Tag car spots with location information
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Provide customer support when requested
  • Comply with legal obligations

AI Processing

When you use the car identification feature:

  • Images are sent to Google Gemini AI for car recognition processing
  • Images are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored on AI servers
  • Results (car make, model, specs) are returned to the app and saved to your profile

Data Storage

Your data is stored as follows:

  • Cloud Storage: Account data, car spots, comments, and likes are stored on Firebase (Google Cloud) servers
  • Photo Storage: Car spot photos are stored on Firebase Storage
  • Data is associated with your account and accessible when you log in from any device
  • Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated data from the app settings

Social Features

Drive Feed includes social features where:

  • Your car spots (photo, car details, location, caption) are visible to all users on the feed
  • Your username and points are visible on the leaderboard
  • Other users can like and comment on your spots
  • You can like and comment on other users' spots

Location Data

We collect location data only when you use the scan feature:

  • Location is captured at the time of scanning to tag where the car was spotted
  • Location is displayed publicly on your car spot posts
  • You can deny location permissions, but you will not be able to use the scan feature
  • We do not track your location in the background

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Authentication is handled securely through Firebase Authentication
  • Data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS)
  • We follow Apple's security guidelines and best practices
  • Access to user data is limited to essential app functionality only

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Limited sharing may occur only in these circumstances:

  • AI Processing: Images are sent to Google Gemini for car recognition
  • Cloud Services: Data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure
  • Public Content: Your car spots, username, and comments are visible to other app users
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete your account and all associated data from app settings
  • Delete individual car spots you have posted
  • Deny camera, photo library, or location permissions at any time via iOS Settings
  • Request information about what data we process
  • Contact us to request data export or deletion

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes:

  • The "Last Updated" date will be revised
  • Significant changes will be communicated through app updates
  • Continued use of our app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy
